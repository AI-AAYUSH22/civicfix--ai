Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

CivicFix AI is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you interact with CivicFix AI, including through WhatsApp and related channels.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information: Your WhatsApp phone number as provided through Meta/WhatsApp.
  • Messages and media: Text messages, photographs, and other content you voluntarily send to us.
  • Location information: Latitude and longitude when you voluntarily share your WhatsApp location or provide location information for a civic complaint.
  • Usage information: Basic service information such as timestamps, report status, and features used.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create, manage, and track civic issue reports such as potholes and road defects.
  • Identify the relevant municipal ward or responsible contractor.
  • Verify issue locations and analyze submitted images using computer-vision and related software.
  • Communicate with you about your report and provide status updates.
  • Improve the reliability, security, and functionality of CivicFix AI.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate the service, including:

  • With authorized municipal authorities or contractors for handling and verifying civic reports.
  • With service providers that support hosting, databases, mapping, communications, or other platform operations.
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect the safety and security of the service.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain complaint information and related evidence only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, verify and track civic issues, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with legal requirements.

6. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about information associated with your civic report or to request correction or deletion where applicable and technically feasible, subject to legal and operational requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

CivicFix AI may rely on third-party services such as Meta/WhatsApp, hosting providers, mapping services, and cloud infrastructure. Their handling of information is also subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the CivicFix AI team using the contact information associated with this application.
